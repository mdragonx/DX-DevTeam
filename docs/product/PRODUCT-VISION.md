# Product Vision

## Product

DX-DevTeam Autonomous Development Control Plane.

## Vision

Enable an autonomous digital engineering organization to transform a requirement into verified, secure, documented, and deployable software with zero routine human interaction.

## Primary user

An owner or organization that needs dependable software delivery while retaining complete visibility, auditability, and policy control.

## Core outcome

The system does not deliver because an agent believes it finished. It delivers only when objective evidence proves that the version satisfies its machine-verifiable specification and mandatory policies.

## Capabilities

- Multichannel requirement intake and normalization.
- Dynamic specialist composition and evaluated expertise.
- Architecture, UX, implementation, testing, security, deployment, and documentation.
- Adversarial self-critique and bounded automatic correction.
- Continuous expertise learning with evaluation and rollback.
- Multi-model routing through OmniRoute.
- Forgejo-centered delivery history.
- Docker Swarm deployment with progressive verification and rollback.
- End-to-end evidence and continuity documentation.

## Non-goals for v0.1

- Unbounded production authority.
- Claims of defect-free software.
- Self-modification of security policies or permissions.
- Silent acceptance of ambiguous high-risk requirements.
- Mixing resources or context with DragonX-Enterprise.
